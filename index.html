What the model assumes

The model adds current to through-water speed to calculate time. Fuel burn combines a fixed hourly auxiliary load with propulsion burn proportional to speed cubed. Six 40-nautical-mile legs have fixed currents and resistance multipliers. Planned leg times round up to the next minute.

Both policies use whole-knot speeds from 9 to 18. The optimizer searches feasible speed sequences on that grid. The constant-speed policy uses the best single speed for every leg. Some savings come from using time the constant-speed plan leaves unused. The model treats route conditions as known and uses an illustrative fuel curve.

Direct CO₂ uses the diesel/gas oil conversion factor of 3.206 tonnes CO₂ per tonne of fuel. IMO resolution MEPC.364(79)
